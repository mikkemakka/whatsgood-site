How What's Good works

  1. Tonight. One question every night: write what was good today and press return. One card is enough.
  2. Journal. Every evening is kept, one card a day, in the order you wrote it.
  3. Map. The people and things you write about gather around you, closer the more they matter. You never tag anything.
  4. Look closer, down to the morning you wrote about. It's all drawn on your iPhone.
