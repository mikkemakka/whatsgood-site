What you need

An iPhone 15 Pro or newer on iOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on (Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri), and writing in English. Apple Intelligence draws the map on your iPhone.

My map is empty

The map needs Apple Intelligence. If it's off, or still downloading after you turned it on, the Map says so. Everything you write is kept, and joins your map once Apple Intelligence is ready. On an iPhone without Apple Intelligence, Tonight and the Journal work just the same.

Will I lose what I've written?

Not when the app updates. But for now, what you write lives only on this iPhone: deleting the app, or moving to a new iPhone without restoring a backup, loses it.

The reminder doesn't come

Check that notifications for What's Good are allowed in Settings › Notifications. The reminder comes 30 minutes before your bedtime, and never on evenings you've already written.

Sending feedback during the beta

Take a screenshot in the app and tap Share Beta Feedback, or open the TestFlight app, tap What's Good, then Send Beta Feedback. You can also write to the address above.

If things feel heavy

What's Good is a habit for noticing what's good, not a medical or mental health service. If things feel heavy, talk to someone you trust or a local helpline. In Norway: Mental Helse, 116 123.
